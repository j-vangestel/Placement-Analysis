Notes & Configuration
Match Type Keywords

Campaigns are classified by match type based on these keywords in their names (case-insensitive):

AutoExactPhrase BroadASIN / PTCAT / Category
Colour Coding

CTR/CVR Green — High performance  |  Yellow — Average  |  Red — Low performance
ACoS Green — Below 30%  |  Yellow — 30–50%  |  Red — Above 50%

🚀 Scale Badge

Rows with ACoS ≤ 12% and ≥ 2 orders are flagged with a 🚀 Scale badge — a signal to consider increasing bids or budget on that placement.

🚨 Alert System — Bleeder & Wasted Spend

Alerts appear in the Alert column of SP and SB Campaigns tabs and are evaluated at placement level independently per row. Only one alert fires per row — Wasted Spend always overrides Bleeder.

🛑 Wasted Spend CRITICAL — Spend ≥ 25 and zero orders. Pure budget waste with no conversions. Consider pausing the placement or reducing bids aggressively.
🚨 Bleeder HIGH — Spend ≥ 15, orders ≤ 1, and ACoS ≥ 60%. Spend is active but return is poor — review bid strategy or negate the placement.

False-positive guard: Rows with fewer than 1,000 impressions are only evaluated if spend ≥ 50, preventing alerts on sparse / low-traffic data. In Aggregated view the group header shows a flagged-placement count; expand the row to see which placements triggered the alert.

Toggle between Flat Rows (one row per placement) and Aggregated (campaigns collapsed into a single summary row). Click ▶ Expand on any campaign row to reveal its individual placement breakdown.
